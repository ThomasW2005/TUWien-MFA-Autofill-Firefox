TUWien-MFA-Autofill

Configure once, then log in automatically on HTTPS TU Wien websites.

A saved secret stays hidden. Leave this field blank to keep it; replacement secrets are visible while you enter them. Use the Base32 secret from your authenticator setup, not a six-digit code. Your device clock must be accurate.

Custom form selectors

Optional CSS selectors for forms the automatic detector does not recognize.

Credentials and the MFA secret are stored locally in this browser profile, without encryption or sync. Anyone with access to the profile can retrieve them. Storing both factors together reduces MFA protection. Only enable this on a device you trust.
